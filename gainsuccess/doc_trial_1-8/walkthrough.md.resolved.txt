# Walkthrough - Stock Indicator Gain Success Analysis

I have completed the analysis to measure the probability of a "gain success" (price increase > 10% after 20 trading days) when a stock indicator is in its top 10th percentile.

## Changes Made

### Logic & Implementation
- **Script**: Developed [code/analyze_gain_success.py](file://gandalf/sm-home/potentials/gainsuccess/code/analyze_gain_success.py).
- **Gain Calculation**: Implemented 21-day price gain (jump 21 daynums) assigned to the starting day.
- **Indicator Thresholding**: Calculates the 90th percentile threshold for each stock individually based on the most recent 132 valid trading days (where both indicator and gain data are present).
- **Special Mapping**: Handled [longi_uptrend.csv](file://gandalf/sm-home/potentials/gainsuccess/input/longi_uptrend.csv) with the specified categorical-to-numerical mapping:
    - `"VeryGood"` / `"Very good"` → 5
    - `"Good"` → 4
    - `"Maybe"` → 2
    - Others → 0

### Data Processing
- Handled European CSV formats (`;` separator, `,` decimal).
- Aligned data across multiple `longi_*.csv` files and the main price file [PotDat.csv](file://gandalf/sm-home/potentials/gainsuccess/input/PotDat.csv).
- Aggregated per-stock probabilities into average, median, highest, and lowest statistics for each indicator.

### Trial 7: Component Analysis (Lift Analysis)
**Question**: How much "lift" does the indicator provide over the baseline probability of a 2% gain?

In this trial (132 days, 90th percentile), we compared the success rate of signalled days against all days in the window.

| Metric | Value | Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Average Base Gain** | **70.86%** | Probability of a >2% max gain occurring on *any* random day. |
| **Average Signal Frequency** | 9.16% | % of days where the indicator exceeds its 90th percentile. |
| **Average Conditional Success**| **86.68%** | Success rate *only* on days when the indicator signals. |

> [!TIP]
> **Indicator Lift**: The `median_40d` indicator provides a **+15.82% improvement** (86.68% - 70.86%) in the probability of achieving a 2% gain. While a 2% gain is already likely (71%), the indicator significantly filters out more "losing" days, bringing the success rate close to 87%.
